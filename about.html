About H Drywall, LLC

30+ years of experience. Licensed, bonded and insured. Serving all of Oahu.

H Drywall, LLC started from humble beginnings and grew into one of the most trusted drywall contractors in Honolulu. We specialize in residential live-in renovations and take pride in treating every home with care, keeping job sites organized and delivering finishes that look exactly right.

Our story

We started out of a 10x10 storage unit and built the business one project at a time through honest work and consistent results. Today H Drywall, LLC operates out of a 2,500 square foot warehouse at 1320 Kalani St Unit 301, Honolulu, HI 96817, and has completed hundreds of residential and commercial projects across Oahu.

With 30+ years of hands-on experience behind the helm, we understand what it takes to do this work correctly the first time. We have earned an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau and top reviews on Angi, Yelp and HomeAdvisor — not through marketing, but through showing up on time, communicating clearly and leaving job sites cleaner than we found them.

Our team is composed of skilled professionals who are dedicated to making damaged and broken things look new, and to building lasting relationships with our clients.
